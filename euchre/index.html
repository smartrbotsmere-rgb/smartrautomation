Rules used here
  • 24-card deck (9 through Ace), partners sit across. You + Partner vs West + East.
  • Right bower (Jack of trump) is highest, then Left bower (other Jack of the same colour — it counts as trump), then A, K, Q, 10, 9 of trump.
  • Round 1: order up the turned card or pass. Dealer picks it up and discards. Round 2: name any other suit or pass. If everyone passes twice, it's a redeal.
  • You must follow the suit led if you can (the Left bower follows trump, not its own suit).
  • Makers take 3–4 tricks = 1 pt · all 5 = 2 pts · all 5 alone = 4 pts. Makers euchred = 2 pts to defenders.
  • Defend alone: when someone goes alone, a defender can go alone too — euchre them solo for 4 pts.
  • Gold dot on a card = trump. First team to 10 wins.
